Part 01 // Multi-Year Periodization

Phased Training Roadmap (Beginner to Advanced)

Select any stage below to inspect the exact split architecture, rep schemes, proximity to failure (RPE/RIR), and progressive overload methodology.

Phase 1 Blueprint: Foundation & Linear Progression

Ideal Split: 3-Day Full-Body (Mon / Wed / Fri or Tue / Thu / Sat)

Goal: Master Form + Add 2.5kg/5lbs Every 1–2 Weeks
Core Focus

Motor-unit recruitment, bracing mechanics, and full range-of-motion mastery on squats, hinges, presses, and rows.

Rep Ranges & Sets

3 working sets per exercise. Compounds: 6–10 reps. Accessories: 10–15 reps.

Intensity Level

RPE 6.5–7.5 (2–3 RIR). Always leave 2–3 clean reps in reserve so form never breaks down.

Progression Method

Linear Progression: Hit top of rep range for all 3 sets, then add 2.5 kg (5 lbs) next session.

Phase 2 Blueprint: Hypertrophy Volume & Double Progression

Ideal Split: 4-Day Upper/Lower or 5-Day Push/Pull/Legs + Upper/Lower

Goal: 12–16 Hard Sets Per Muscle Group / Week
Core Focus

Accumulating mechanical tension across all muscle heads by pairing heavy compounds with targeted isolation lifts (lateral raises, curls, leg curls).

Rep Ranges & Sets

3–4 working sets. Primary Strength: 5–8 reps. Hypertrophy & Isolation: 8–15 reps.

Intensity Level

RPE 8–9 (1–2 RIR) on compounds; final set of isolation exercises taken safely to 0–1 RIR.

Progression Method

Double Progression: Work within an 8–12 rep window. Once you hit 3×12 with strict form, increase load by 2.5–5% and reset to 3×8.

Phase 3 Blueprint: Golden Era Specialization & Periodization

Ideal Split: 5–6 Day Specialized Split (e.g., Arnold Split: Chest/Back, Shoulders/Arms, Legs ×2)

Goal: Weak-Point Mastery & Fatigue Management
Core Focus

Bringing up lagging body parts (e.g., rear delts, upper chest, calves) through prioritization at the start of sessions and higher frequency.

Intensity Techniques

Rest-Pause sets, Drop Sets, Myo-Reps, and lengthened partials applied selectively on the final set of isolation exercises.

Periodization Blocks

6–8 week accumulation blocks ramping volume from MEV to MRV, followed by a 1-week Deload (50% volume) to supercompensate.

Intensity Level

RPE 8.5–10 (0–1.5 RIR) with strict logbook tracking of total tonnage and rep PRs.

Nutrient Timing & Peri-Workout Protocol

1–2 Hours Pre-Workout

Prime Glycogen & Prevent Catabolism

Consume 30–45g lean protein + 45–75g easily digestible complex carbs with low fat/fiber so digestion doesn't slow blood flow to working muscles.

Example: Chicken breast + white rice + 1 banana, OR Whey isolate + oats + berries.
Within 60–90 Mins Post-Workout

Spike Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS)

Consume 35–50g fast-absorbing protein + 50–90g carbohydrates to replenish depleted muscle glycogen and lower post-training cortisol.

Example: Lean beef or salmon + baked potatoes/rice, OR Whey shake + rice cakes & honey.
